talkback

Open source practice tool. Your key and files stay in this browser and go only to the AI provider you pick.

Solve Math

Type a problem or attach a photo of one. Ask follow-ups in the same thread.

Interview Answers

Paste any interview question and get a strong answer. Answers use your resume and job description if you added them under Mock Interview.

Mock Interview

Upload your resume and the job description. An AI interviewer asks tailored questions one at a time, gives feedback on each answer, and ends with a scored report.

Live Assist

Listens to your call, writes down who said what, and suggests an answer when the other side asks a question. Answers use your documents, resume and job description. Needs a Groq key in Settings; without one it hears only your microphone. Use it only where AI help is allowed, and tell the people on the call.

Settings

talkback has no server. It calls the AI provider directly from your browser using your own key.

Stored only in this browser's local storage. Not needed for a local server like Ollama.
Lets Live Assist hear the call itself and label who spoke. Free key from console.groq.com. Call audio is sent to Groq to be transcribed.
